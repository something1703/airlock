Clause 14.2 — Early termination. Tenant (Maya Chen) may terminate this Lease before the End Date by giving Landlord (Patel Property Group LLC, 220 Lakeview Ave, Suite 400) not less than sixty (60) days' written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to two (2) months' Base Rent, being $3,400.00, together with any unamortized concessions. The Security Deposit shall not be applied toward the early termination fee.
